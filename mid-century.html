Mid-Century Modern Guide

1940s–1960s design emphasizing clean lines, function-first forms, and organic silhouettes in teak, walnut, molded plywood, fiberglass, and brass accents.

What Collectors Look For

Maker labels, joinery quality, proportion, and surface originality.

Quality Clues

  • Solid wood details and veneers
  • Consistent hardware and patina
  • Documented designer/manufacturer context
